Arthur Edelstein ni mhandisi wa programu anayezingatia ustawi wa binadamu. Katika Tor, alianzisha stream isolation, ambapo Tor Browser hutumia [mzunguko tofauti kwa kila tovuti unayotembelea](https://support.torproject.org/tor-browser/features/managing-identities/#:~:text=The%20URL%20bar). Katika Firefox, alikuwa meneja wa bidhaa wa timu zilizotengeneza [Total Cookie Protection](https://blog.mozilla.org/security/2021/02/23/total-cookie-protection/) na [HTTPS-Only Mode](https://blog.mozilla.org/security/2020/11/17/firefox-83-introduces-https-only-mode/). Katika Brave, alianzisha [HTTPS by Default](https://brave.com/privacy-updates/22-https-by-default/) na alishiriki kuandika karatasi kuhusu [Pool-Party Attack](https://brave.com/research/files/pool-party-usenix-2023.pdf) kwenye faragha. Mwaka 2021, alichapisha [PrivacyTests.org](https://privacytests.org), mradi wa kuwawajibisha watengenezaji wa vivinjari vya wavuti kwa uvujaji wao wa taarifa za faragha. Pia alishiriki kuunda [Project Timestamper](https://projecttimestamper.org), juhudi inayoendelea ya kuhifadhi uhalisi wa maarifa na utamaduni wa binadamu katika enzi ya AI.
